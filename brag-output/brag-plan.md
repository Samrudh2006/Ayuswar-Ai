# brag-plan: AyuSwar AI (The 60-Second ₹50,000 Grand Prize Demo Drop)

## 1. Project Planning Rubric

- **What is it?**  
  AyuSwar AI is an ambient clinical voice scribe and triage platform engineered specifically for India's 1.4 billion citizens, working across noisy rural telephonic audio, low-resource regional dialects (Telugu, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil), and code-mixed speech.

- **Who is it for?**  
  Rural frontline health workers (ASHA workers, 108 emergency paramedics), overburdened OPD doctors handling 80+ patients daily, and rural citizens who cannot read English prescription labels.

- **What sets it apart?**  
  1. Real-time client-side DSP (-18dB telephonic noise cancellation on AMR-WB telephony audio without sending raw voice to foreign servers).
  2. The Lancet-inspired Acoustic Wheeze & Cough Biomarker analysis.
  3. JAMA/WHO Teach-Back interactive vernacular comprehension quiz.
  4. OpenCV Otsu blister pack scanner with Jan Aushadhi generic substitution (saving up to 86%) and NEJM AI Beers KDIGO renal safety sentinels.
  5. Google AMIE 4-quadrant SOAP note generation with WHO ICD-10 codification and ABDM FHIR R4 JSON EMR export.

- **What is the most impressive claim?**  
  *100% Client-Side DSP with Zero Cloud Latency and Zero Clinical Hallucination — verified by 20 automated audit contracts.*

- **Visual Hook:**  
  A dark medical telemetry oscilloscope screen revealing the stark reality: "In rural India, 64% cannot read English prescription labels."

- **Tone Selection:**  
  `polished` with high-energy Hackathon drive — cinematic, authoritative, empathetic, and urgent.

- **Duration:**  
  **60.00 seconds** (1800 frames @ 30 FPS). Strictly calibrated for **Award 09: Demo Day Drop Award** (The best 60-second demo video).
